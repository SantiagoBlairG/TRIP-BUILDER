Country and trip cards use these country-level inspiration photographs with captions naming the actual subject. City cards use the 24 separate images documented in [city photo credits](cities/CREDITS.md). Activity cards reuse their own city's photograph as labeled city inspiration, rather than claiming to depict each venue or activity.

The reproducible downloader is `scripts/download-images.mjs`. Normal installation, tests, and builds do not need to run it.
